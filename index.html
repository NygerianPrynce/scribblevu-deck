FAQ · HOW SCRIBBLE USES AI
The AI builds the experiment.
The student does the thinking.
1

Predict before explain

Scribble asks for a bet before every race and explains only after.

2

Grounded in the lesson

Answers come from the teacher's own curriculum, not the open web.

3

Voice stays on device

Whisper transcribes locally. Raw audio is never uploaded.

4

Always labeled AI

Students always know Scribble is an AI guide.

5

Trends, not surveillance

Teachers see class-level misconceptions to plan the next lesson.

6

Teacher stays in charge

They pick the curriculum and see everything that was generated.
